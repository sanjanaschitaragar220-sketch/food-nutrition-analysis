HOW IT WORKS

How the AI Nutrition Pipeline Works

A multi-stage pipeline designed for instant dietary estimations from food photography.

01

Object Detection & Segmentation

Scans the image matrix for culinary geometry, separating the foreground plate from background artifacts.

02

Classification & Volumetrics

Identifies multi-label food classes (proteins, carbs, lipids) and estimates relative portion mass based on depth.

03

Nutrient Database Mapping

Cross-references identified food items against verified nutritional datasets (USDA FoodData Central / McCance & Widdowson).

04

Real-time Macro Synthesis

Generates final calorie metrics, macronutrient split (Protein/Carb/Fat), micronutrients, and health scores.
